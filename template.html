Company Introduction

Building a bridge for friendly trade between Korea and the world.

PAYTTO was established in 2013 and is headquartered in Goyang-si, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea. It is a comprehensive international trading and industrial solutions provider — importing and exporting electric vehicles and related components, delivering complete vehicle assembly plant solutions, and installing EV charging infrastructure alongside photovoltaic power and new-energy solutions.

Beyond mobility, PAYTTO trades electronics, cosmetics, health supplements and food products, operates cross-border e-commerce and live-streaming businesses, and conducts international trade of raw materials such as aluminum, copper, gold, coal and crude oil — while advancing used-battery recycling to build a full-circle industrial system across new energy, trade, and resource circulation.

Resources & New Energy

Trade, recycle, regenerate.

A closed-loop approach spanning raw materials trade, used-battery recycling, and solar power generation.

Raw Materials

Aluminum ingots, copper cathodes, coal and gold — traded with efficient circulation and high quality standards, including aluminum from Cameroon and copper from Gabon.

Battery Recycling

Recycling and reutilization of used EV batteries — reducing dependence on raw material imports and supporting a sustainable supply chain.

Solar Power

Solar power plant projects in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, plus a solar-powered aquaculture project — each targeting 100 MW installations.
